How it works

The problem we solve

Ride apps were built for dense cities. In a mid-size city there are fewer drivers, so waits are long and prices swing wildly with demand. Drivers earn less, so many quit, and the waits get longer. Riders who need a car for work or a doctor's visit cannot plan around that.

Our answer

Hoplane is a marketplace that connects riders with local, independent drivers. We set a fixed price from distance and time of day, and we plan driver shifts around the demand we expect, so a car is nearby when people need it.

1. Book

Enter where you are going and see the price.

2. Ride

A nearby driver picks you up, usually within eight minutes.

3. Pay

Pay by card in the app. The driver keeps 80 percent of the fare.
