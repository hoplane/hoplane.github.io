Now in Columbus, Dayton and Cincinnati

In a big city you wait three minutes for a car. In a mid-size city you can wait twenty, and then the price jumps because it is raining. We think that is backwards. Hoplane shows you one price before you book, and that is the price you pay, at rush hour, in the rain, after the game.
