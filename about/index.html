Our story

Daniel Reyes and Priya Natarajan started Hoplane in June 2024. They met two years before that, in 2022, at a city transit hackathon in Cleveland, where their team built a bus-tracking tool in one weekend. They have built things together ever since.

Daniel had spent years running bus routes and knew where people were left waiting. Priya had built routing software for freight trucks and knew how to make the numbers work. Hoplane is what came out of that.

Our promise

Fair prices for riders, a fair share for drivers, and a ride that shows up in the cities that are usually an afterthought.
